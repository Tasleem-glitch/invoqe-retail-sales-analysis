# Invoqe Retail Sales Analysis and Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing retail sales data to identify business trends, understand profitability, and present key findings through a sales dashboard.

Completed as part of the Invoqe Data Analyst Internship using Google Sheets on a mobile device.

## Objectives

- Analyse retail sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales across product categories and regions.
- Understand customer segment performance.
- Evaluate shipping mode performance.
- Examine discount levels and profitability.
- Present findings through charts and business insights.

## Dataset

- Dataset: Sample Superstore Dataset
- Records: 9,994
- Source: Kaggle
- Data type: Retail sales transaction data

## Tools Used

- Google Sheets
- Spreadsheet formulas
- Charts and data visualization

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Sales: 2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: 286,397.02
- Total Records: 9,994
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.47%

## Analysis Performed

1. Overall sales and profit analysis.
2. Category-wise analysis.
3. Region-wise analysis.
4. Customer segment analysis.
5. Sub-category analysis.
6. Ship mode analysis.
7. Discount-level analysis.

## Dashboard Visualizations

Five column charts:

1. Sales by Category
2. Profit by Category
3. Sales by Region
4. Sales by Customer Segment
5. Sales by Ship Mode

## Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales and profit among categories.
- West recorded the highest total sales and profit among regions.
- Consumer customers contributed the highest sales.
- Home Office had the highest customer-segment profit margin.
- Standard Class shipping generated the highest sales and profit.
- Discount levels of 30% and above were associated with negative total profit.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 12.47%.
